🎧 GUIDE · PORTATUNE

  • 1️⃣ SAVE FILES FIRST
    Download audio to your device. From WhatsApp: tap & hold → "Save to Files".
  • 2️⃣ IMPORT
    Click "IMPORT LOCAL MUSIC" and select your files (multiple at once).
  • 3️⃣ LOOP MODES
    🔁 Repeat all playlist. 🔂 Repeat one song. No loop = stops after each track.
  • 🎛️ EQ
    Press "EQ ON" to expand equalizer. Choose presets or adjust sliders.
  • 🎨 COLORS
    Use the small circles (top right) to change the interface theme.
  • 📱 ADD TO HOME SCREEN
    Share menu → "Add to Home Screen" → standalone app.
  • 📝 MANAGE PLAYLIST
    Use ↑ ↓ to reorder tracks. Click ❌ to remove a song.
  • ⌨️ KEYBOARD
    ← → move between buttons. ↑ ↓ navigate playlist. Enter to play.
